Our Story

A hub for treats that actually taste homemade

Blissbite Hub started with one tray of puff puff and one pot of zobo, sold from a single counter to neighbours who kept coming back. That same standard still runs the kitchen: real ingredients, short ingredient lists, and nothing pre-made that could be made fresh instead.

Today the counter carries a full spread of savoury pastries, pies and cakes, alongside three drinks brewed the traditional way, zobo steeped with ginger and cloves, tigernut blended smooth, and kunu spiced and chilled. Order ahead and we'll deliver anywhere in Lagos.
